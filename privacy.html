Privacy policy

UTC to Local Time
Effective date: November 25, 2026

UTC to Local Time is a browser extension published by an independent developer. This policy describes what the extension does with information on your device. It applies to the extension distributed through the Chrome Web Store and Microsoft Edge Add-ons.

What the extension does

The extension looks at text on pages you visit, finds times written in UTC, and rewrites those times into your local time. If you click the toolbar icon, it can highlight a time it missed so you can convert that text. A control on that panel can switch between 12-hour and 24-hour display, or pause conversion on the current site.

Information that stays on your device

Page text is processed in your browser to find and replace UTC times. That text is not sent to the developer or to any server.

The extension stores two settings in your browser with the extension storage API:

  • whether times are shown in 12-hour or 24-hour form
  • the sites where you have paused conversion

Those settings remain on your device. They are not sold, shared, or uploaded. Uninstalling the extension, or removing its data in your browser’s extension settings, deletes them.

Information the developer does not collect

The developer does not collect your name, email address, location, browsing history, account credentials, or payment information. The extension has no account, no analytics, and no advertising. It does not send page content, settings, or any other user data to the developer or to a third party.

Permissions

The extension asks to read and change web pages so it can replace UTC times on the sites you visit. It asks to store data so it can remember your display choice and paused sites. It asks to run a script when you click the toolbar icon so it can highlight a missed time on the open page. These permissions are used only for that purpose.

Children

The extension is a general time-conversion tool. It is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
